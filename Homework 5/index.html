Integration: Riemann, Lebesgue and the Normal CDF

Exercise 1 – Riemann and Lebesgue integration

Nine functions, including discontinuous, unbounded and pathological ones (Dirichlet, Thomae). Riemann sums vs. range-partition (Lebesgue-style) sums: accuracy, convergence, cost.

Exercise 2 – Numerical computation of the normal CDF

Trapezoid, Simpson, power series, erf approximations and a hybrid series/continued-fraction method, checked against SciPy reference values. Liouville's theorem and the role of erf.
