Welford’s Recurrence Formulas and Sampling Statistics

Introduction

Computing the mean and variance of a data stream naively requires storing all the data or using the formula Σx² − k·M², which suffers from numerical cancellation. Online (recurrence) algorithms update the statistics one observation at a time, using constant memory and remaining numerically stable.

Mk = Mk−1 + (xk − Mk−1)/k  (Knuth)
SSk = SSk−1 + (xk − Mk−1)(xk − Mk)  (Welford)

This homework has two parts: first we derive Welford’s formulas and implement them; then we use them in a simulation of m hackers attacking n systems, to study the behaviour of the sampling mean and sampling variance and why they matter in inferential statistics.
